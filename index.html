03 / Mobile Cytology

Clinical screening that fits in a pocket.

Lightweight distilled models run entirely on a smartphone, turning a direct image input into a structured interpretation and clinical report—with no cloud dependency or recurring infrastructure cost.

Built for

Decentralized screening, rural clinics and point-of-care settings where cytopathology expertise may be limited.

04 / Vision Assist

Ask what’s around you. Hear what matters.

A hands-free AI companion for people who are blind or have low vision. Users simply speak to their phone to understand their surroundings, identify what is ahead and ask follow-up questions—without finding or pressing a button.

About HealForge

Research-led.
Care-focused.

HealForge AI is a healthcare and MedTech startup founded by faculty and researchers at NIT Calicut. We translate multidisciplinary research into intelligent tools for healthcare professionals, hospitals and patients.

Our vision

To make AI-powered MedTech more accessible—enabling better diagnosis, monitoring, clinical workflows and patient care.

Our work sits at the intersection of clinical needs, artificial intelligence and product engineering. That intersection keeps our technology ambitious, but always anchored in real-world usefulness.
